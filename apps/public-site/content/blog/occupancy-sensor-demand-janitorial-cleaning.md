## The Flaw of the Fixed Cleaning Schedule

In traditional commercial real estate, janitorial schedules are set in stone:
- *10:00 AM: Restroom check*
- *2:00 PM: Restroom check*
- *6:00 PM: Nightly deep clean*

This calendar-based model has a major flaw: **Human foot traffic isn't uniform**.

On a Tuesday after a major 3rd-floor conference, a restroom might see 180 visitors by 11:30 AM — leaving soap empty, trash overflowing, and counter surfaces soiled hours before the scheduled 2:00 PM check. Meanwhile, a quiet Friday afternoon sees zero visitors, yet a day porter spends 20 minutes "cleaning" an untouched restroom.

For tech-leaning facility managers, **demand-driven janitorial cleaning** solves this imbalance.

## How Occupancy-Based Janitorial Telemetry Works

Instead of cleaning on a fixed clock, IoT occupancy sensors track real-time facility usage:

```
[Infrared Door Counter / Stall Sensor]
                 │
                 ▼ (Reaches threshold: e.g., 100 entries)
[Automated Dispatch Trigger Sent to Day Porter]
                 │
                 ▼
[Targeted Clean Executed & NFC Verified]
```

### The 3 Core Sensor Types:

1. **Passive Infrared (PIR) Overhead Door Counters**: Mounted above restroom entryways to count total entries and exits.
2. **Stall-Door Magnetic Micro-Switches**: Tracks exact toilet/urinal stall uses to trigger localized sanitization.
3. **Time-of-Flight (ToF) Optical Grid Sensors**: High-accuracy spatial sensors that measure occupancy density without violating privacy or capturing PII.
