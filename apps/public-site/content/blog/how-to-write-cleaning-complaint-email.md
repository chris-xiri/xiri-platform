## The Frustration of Unperformed Janitorial Work

Few things frustrate a property manager, practice administrator, or office director more than arriving at 8:00 AM to find unemptied trash cans, sticky restroom floors, or un-dusted conference tables.

Your first instinct isn't to fire your janitorial vendor immediately — canceling a contract and finding a replacement takes time and effort. Instead, you want to **remedy the relationship** and give your cleaning company a chance to fix the quality issues.

However, sending a vague email like *"The office looks dirty, please clean better"* rarely produces lasting results. 

To get your commercial cleaning vendor to take immediate action, you need **specific, SLA-focused communication**.

Below are three copy-paste B2B email templates for escalating quality issues, along with the industry reason why feedback alone often fails.

---

## Template 1: Gentle Reminder (First Notice)
*Use this when quality issues first appear — e.g., a missed trash can or un-stocked paper towels on a single night.*

> **Subject:** Quality Note – Service Walkthrough for [FACILITY ADDRESS] – [DATE]
>
> Dear [ACCOUNT MANAGER NAME],
>
> I hope you’re having a great week. I’m writing to share a quick operational note regarding last night’s cleaning service at [FACILITY ADDRESS].
>
> During our morning walkthrough, we noticed the following items were missed:
> - [Item 1: e.g., Trash cans in the 2nd-floor East wing were not emptied]
> - [Item 2: e.g., Restroom soap dispensers on the main floor were left un-stocked]
>
> We value our partnership with [CLEANING COMPANY NAME] and want to make sure your night crew has the details needed to keep our building in top shape. Could you please review these scope items with the team before tonight’s shift?
>
> Thank you for your quick attention to this!
>
> Best regards,  
> **[YOUR NAME]**  
> [YOUR TITLE] | [YOUR COMPANY NAME]

---

## Template 2: Formal Quality Complaint (Repeated Issues)
*Use this when issues persist after 2 or 3 reminders, or when multiple building areas are neglected.*

> **Subject:** Formal Service SLA Notice – Quality Issues at [FACILITY ADDRESS]
>
> Dear [ACCOUNT MANAGER NAME],
>
> I am writing to formally log a recurring quality issue regarding the janitorial service provided by [CLEANING COMPANY NAME] at [FACILITY ADDRESS].
>
> Despite previous notes sent on [PAST DATE 1] and [PAST DATE 2], the following Scope of Work (SOW) items remain unfulfilled:
> 1. **Restroom Sanitization**: Countertops and stall partitions show visible buildup.
> 2. **High Dusting**: Supply air vents and light fixtures have not been dusted in [NUMBER] weeks.
> 3. **Floor Care**: Hardwood/tile surfaces in the lobby have not been mopped properly.
>
> As a commercial facility, clean and compliant spaces are essential for our operations and tenant satisfaction. We require a written response detailing how [CLEANING COMPANY NAME] will rectify these deficiencies by [DATE - e.g., 48 hours].
>
> We request an immediate supervisor audit of tonight’s shift to confirm these items are resolved.
>
> Sincerely,  
> **[YOUR NAME]**  
> [YOUR TITLE] | [YOUR COMPANY NAME]

---

## Template 3: Request for Operations Walkthrough (Pre-Cancellation Warning)
*Use this as a final step before initiating formal contract cancellation or issuing an RFP.*

> **Subject:** Urgent: Required Site Walkthrough & Operations Review – [FACILITY ADDRESS]
>
> Dear [ACCOUNT MANAGER NAME / OWNER NAME],
>
> Due to ongoing non-performance and unfulfilled Scope of Work items at [FACILITY ADDRESS], we are requesting an urgent on-site walkthrough with your Operations Director.
>
> We have documented multiple instances of missed cleaning tasks, unverified shifts, and non-compliance with our agreed SLA. At this time, our executive team is evaluating our facility vendor contracts for the upcoming quarter.
>
> Please provide your availability for an on-site walkthrough on [OPTION DATE 1] or [OPTION DATE 2] so we can review our building expectations in person.
>
> Sincerely,  
> **[YOUR NAME]**  
> [YOUR TITLE] | [YOUR COMPANY NAME]

## Why Feedback Emails Rarely Fix Traditional Cleaning Vendors

If you have sent two or three complaint emails and quality improves for two days before slipping again, you are experiencing the structural flaw of the traditional cleaning industry:

```
[You Send Email Complaint] ──> [Owner Scolds Cleaning Crew] ──> [Crew Cleans Better for 48 Hours]
                                                                        │
                                                                        ▼
                                                         [No Night Supervisor Checks]
                                                                        │
                                                                        ▼
                                                         [Quality Drops Back to Baseline]
```

Traditional cleaning companies rely on **self-reporting**. The person cleaning your building at 10:00 PM is the same person filling out the paper sign-in sheet on the wall. If nobody physically inspects their work at 11:00 PM, feedback emails simply cause temporary anxiety without changing the operational system.
