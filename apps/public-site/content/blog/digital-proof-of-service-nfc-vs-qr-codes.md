## The "Ghost Cleaning" Problem

If you rely on a paper clipboard hanging behind a restroom door or a paper binder in the main lobby, you are vulnerable to **ghost cleaning**.

Ghost cleaning occurs when a janitorial crew checks off tasks that were never performed — pencil-whipping 5:00 PM checks at 1:00 PM, or signing for high dusting that hasn't happened in months.

For tech-leaning property managers and building owners, paper logs create three critical risks:
1. **Zero Real-Time Visibility**: You don't know if a shift was missed until a tenant complains.
2. **Falsified Audit Trails**: Paper clipboards offer zero legal protection during tenant slip-and-fall claims or state health inspections.
3. **No Metric Tracking**: Paper data cannot be analyzed to optimize shift hours or cleaning frequency.

The solution is **Digital Proof of Service**. But should you choose **NFC Tags** or **QR Codes**?

## NFC Tags vs. QR Codes: Which Verification Technology Wins?

```
[Cleaner Arrives at Checkpoint] ──> [Scans NFC Tag / QR Code] ──> [GPS & Timestamp Logged] ──> [Portal Updated]
```

| Evaluation Criteria | Near-Field Communication (NFC) | Quick Response (QR) Codes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Tamper Resistance** | **High**: Embedded encrypted microchip behind wall plate | **Low**: Can be photocopied, damaged, or scanned from a photo |
| **Scan Friction** | **Zero**: Cleaner simply taps mobile device against wall | **Medium**: Cleaner must open camera, align frame, and focus |
| **Location Verification** | **100% Unalterable**: Requires physical contact within 2 cm | **Vulnerable**: Cleaner can scan a photo of the code from off-site |
| **Durability** | **Extreme**: Waterproof, chemical-resistant industrial casing | **Moderate**: Paper/sticker fades, peels, or smudges from cleaning chemicals |
| **Hardware Unit Cost** | $1.50 – $4.00 per physical tag | $0.05 – $0.50 per sticker print |

### Why NFC Tags Outperform QR Codes for Facility Auditing
While QR codes are cheaper to print, they suffer from a major security flaw: **a cleaner can photograph the QR code and scan it from their car or home**. 

NFC (Near-Field Communication) tags operate via short-range electromagnetic fields (under 2 cm). A cleaner *must* physically stand at the exact restroom stall, mechanical room, or suite door to log the task.
