## Indoor Air Quality Is the New Tenant Retention Metric

Post-2020, indoor air quality (IAQ) evolved from a background engineering metric into a primary tenant demand.

Commercial tenants, medical practice managers, and school boards routinely ask building owners:
- *"What is the CO2 concentration in our conference rooms during peak meetings?"*
- *"Are your janitorial crews using low-VOC, Green Seal certified cleaning products?"*
- *"How often are MERV 13 HVAC filters physically inspected and replaced?"*

For tech-leaning facility managers, monitoring IAQ and maintaining ASHRAE 62.1 ventilation standards requires a dual approach: **IoT Air Quality Sensors** + **Verified Preventive Maintenance**.

---

## Understanding IAQ Telemetry Metrics

Smart IAQ sensor monitors (such as Kaiterra, Awair, or Airthings for Business) track five critical parameters:

| IAQ Telemetry Metric | Target Healthy Range | Operational Impact & Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbon Dioxide ($CO_2$)** | < 800 ppm | High $CO_2$ causes drowsiness and cognitive decline; indicates insufficient outdoor air intake or clogged filters. |
| **Total Volatile Organic Compounds (tVOC)** | < 300 ppb | Off-gassing from harsh chemical cleaners or building materials; mitigated via Green Seal GS-37 low-VOC janitorial products. |
| **Particulate Matter ($PM_{2.5}$ / $PM_{10}$)** | < 12 $\mu g/m^3$ | Fine dust, pollen, and airborne particles; controlled via MERV 13+ filtration and HEPA-vacuum stack dusting. |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | 30% – 50% RH | High humidity (>60%) promotes mold and microbial growth; low humidity (<30%) increases viral persistence. |
| **Airflow Differential Pressure ($\Delta P$)** | Specified per HVAC zone | Measures pressure drop across filter banks; alerts when filters are loaded and require immediate replacement. |

## Why IAQ Sensors Fail Without Managed Execution

Installing $200 IAQ sensors in every floor zone gives you continuous data. But data alone doesn't change a dirty MERV 13 filter or remove dust trapped on high book stacks and light fixtures.

> **Data tells you air quality is poor. Managed execution is what cleans the air.**

### The 3 Core Pillars of High IAQ:

1. **Scheduled MERV 13 Filter PM**: Filter replacement executed on a strict calendar, not delayed until motors strain.
2. **Low-VOC Green Cleaning**: Eradicating toxic chemical off-gassing by enforcing Green Seal (GS-37/GS-40) eco-cleaning standards.
3. **HEPA Micro-Dusting**: Using sealed HEPA-vacuum filtration for all high-dusting (shelves, supply vents, return grilles) so dust is captured rather than recirculated.
