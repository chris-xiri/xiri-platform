## The Hidden Cost of "Stub Roll Waste"

If you manage a commercial office building, school campus, or medical facility, look inside your trash cans on a Friday afternoon.

Chances are, you'll find hundreds of feet of perfectly usable paper towels and half-empty toilet tissue rolls.

Why? Because traditional janitorial crews are trained to replace partially used rolls before the weekend to prevent Monday morning tenant complaints. Industry estimates call this **"Stub Roll Waste"**, and it inflates commercial restroom supply costs by **18% to 30% annually**.

For tech-leaning facility managers, **smart dispenser telemetry** and **automated supply chain integration** eliminate this waste entirely.

---

## How Smart Consumable Telemetry Works

Smart paper towel, tissue, and soap dispensers use embedded optical or mechanical sensors to measure remaining capacity in real-time.

```
[Smart Dispenser Sensor] ──> [Cloud Telemetry Portal] ──> [Automated Reorder / Dispatch Trigger]
```

1. **Precision Depletion Thresholds**: Instead of replacing a roll at 35% capacity, smart dispensers send a targeted alert only when capacity reaches <10%.
2. **Dual-Roll Auto-Transfer**: Modern smart dispensers hold a primary roll and a secondary stub roll. The dispenser automatically transfers to the new roll only when the stub roll is 100% depleted.
3. **Automated Consumable Reordering**: Telemetry software tracks monthly consumption rates per floor and automatically triggers reorders with your janitorial distributor before inventory runs dry.
