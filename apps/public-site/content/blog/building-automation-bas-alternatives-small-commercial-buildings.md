## The $100,000 BAS Dilemma for Small Commercial Buildings

If you own or manage a 15,000 to 45,000 sq ft commercial building, medical office park, or retail center, you face a common dilemma.

Enterprise Building Automation Systems (BAS / BMS) from vendors like Honeywell, Johnson Controls, or Siemens offer incredible promises: centralized HVAC monitoring, automated lighting controls, leak detection, and maintenance alerts.

The problem? **Installation costs run $50,000 to $250,000+**, plus expensive annual software licenses and dedicated engineering staff.

For small-to-mid commercial properties, a traditional BAS rarely achieves a reasonable payback period.

The good news? **You can achieve 90% of a BAS's operational benefits at 10% of the cost** using modern low-cost alternatives.

## The Real Goal: Eliminating Emergency OpEx Spikes

The true purpose of building automation isn't installing shiny software dashboards — it is **making building operations predictable, compliant, and emergency-free**.

### What Actually Causes Emergency Capital Spikes in Small Buildings?
1. **Unchecked Condensate Drain Leaks**: A $40 unclogged condensate line causes $25,000 in ceiling and dry-wall water damage.
2. **Neglected HVAC Filters**: Loaded filters strain compressor motors, causing $8,000 premature unit burnouts.
3. **Inconsistent Cleaning Verification**: Unmonitored crews miss high dusting and floor care, leading to tenant non-renewals.
